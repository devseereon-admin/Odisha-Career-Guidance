1 CAT The Common Admission Test (CAT) is national-level entrance examination, which permits aspirants to get into top management institutes like IIMs.
  • Eligibility: Bachelor’s degree with 50% aggregate (45% aggregate or equivalent for reserved categories)
  • Link: : https://iimcat.ac.in/
  • Frequency: Once in a year in November
2 MAT Management Aptitude Test (MAT) is a standardised National Level test being administered since 1988 to facilitate Business Schools (B-Schools) to screen candidates for admission to MBA and allied programmes. MAT is one of the most accepted entrance exams in top MAT accepting colleges in India. Amity University Noida, LPU, Jaipuria Noida, SOA University, Graphic Era Deemed to be University, MNIT Jaipur, NIT Calicut, Birla Institute of Technology, Manipal University, Jaipuria Jaipur, etc. consider this test for admission.
  • Eligibility: Graduates in any discipline. Final year students of Graduate Courses can also apply.
  • Link: https://mat.aima.in/
  • Frequency: Four times a year - February, May, September, December
3 XAT Xavier Aptitude Test or XAT is a national-level management entrance examination conducted by XLRI, Jamshedpur for admission to MBA/PGDM programmes. Apart from XLRI and 10 other XAMI member colleges, over 1,000 B-schools accept XAT scores for admission to their post-graduate management programmes.
  • Eligibility: Recognized Bachelor's degree of minimum three years duration or equivalent in any discipline.
  • Link:"https://www.xatonline.in/"
  • Frequency: Once a year in January
4 SNAP SNAP is an MBA entrance exam in India conducted by Symbiosis International University (SIU). The exam is the gateway to admission into one of the 15 colleges affiliated to SIU. Candidates qualifying the exam are eligible to get admission to 24 MBA/PGDM courses offered by the university.
  • Eligibility: a Bachelor's degree from a recognised university with at least 50 % aggregate (45% for SC / ST) or equivalent.
  • Link: "https://www.snaptest.org/"
  • Frequency: Once a year in December
5 JEE Main & JEE Advanced The JEE (Joint Entrance Examination) is an entrance exam for admission into various engineering colleges across the country.

    JEE Main is an entry-level exam with a lower level of difficulty. It comprises two papers.
      Paper 1 is conducted for admission to Undergraduate Engineering Programs at NITs, IIITs, CFTIs and other prestigious institutions. It is also an eligibility test for JEE Advanced.
      Paper 2 is conducted for admission to B. Arch. and B. Planning courses.
      JEE Advanced is a national level entrance exam which is conducted for admissions to IITs.
    • Eligibility : Class 12th Science with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics /equivalent with a minimum aggregate of 75% for general (65% for SC/ST).
    • Link:"https://jeemain.nta.ac.in/"
      • & "https://jeemain.nta.ac.in/"
    • Frequency: Twice a year JEE Mains is conducted in 2 phases in January and April. There is usually an intermediate period of 50 days after the JEE Main exam before students appear for the JEE Advanced exam.

It also opens the gateway to booming public sector organisations (PSUs) and in the field of research. Some of the PSUs and research organizations which use GATE score for providing jobs include ONGC, NTPC, GAIL, HPCL, PGCIL, BHEL, BSNL, NHPC, BARC, DRDO, etc.
  • Eligibility: Candidates who are studying in 3rd or higher year of an undergraduate program or have completed any government-approved degree program in Engineering/ Technology/ Science/ Architecture/ Humanities are eligible to appear for GATE.
  • Link:"https://gate.iitkgp.ac.in/"
  • Frequency- Once a year in February
