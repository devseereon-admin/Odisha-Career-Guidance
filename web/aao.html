Assistant Agriculture Officer (AAO)

An Assistant Agriculture Officer (AAO) assists in putting agricultural policies and programmes into action, supports and mentors farmers, and advocates for agricultural practices within their block. Usually, they are employed by the government or by agricultural extension agencies.

Skills Required:
  • Communication Skills
  • Problem-solving
  • Technical Proficiency
  • Teamwork
  • Organizational Skills
SUCCESS STORY

Dr. Himanshu Pathak

Dr. Pathak. He is a soil scientist turned secretary at GoI. He is an Assistant Agriculture Officer in Himachal Pradesh and championed the transition to organic horticulture. He guided farmers in adopting organic practices for growing apples and other fruits. The shift not only improved the quality of produce but also opened up new markets for organic fruits, enhancing the economic prospects of the region.

Entrepreneurship

State government launched the scheme “Agriculture Entrepreneurship Promotion Scheme 2018”, on 13th November 2018 to groom at least 1000 rural Agriculture Entrepreneurs (AEs) in coming three years to provide quality agricultural inputs and technological services including modern farm equipment and marketing assurances to farmers of the state. The scheme was launched by the Hon’ble Chief Minister, Odisha during Agri Business Management session at the Make in Odisha Conclave 2018

For The Differently Abled

People with the following disabilities can work in this career
  • OL = one leg affected (R and/or L)
  • OA = one arm affected (R or L)
  • BL = both legs affected
  • HH = Hearing Handicapped (persons suffering from hearing impairment)
  • Low vision
  • Leprosy cured persons
