Psephologist

Psephology is a division of political science that deals with the examination as well as the statistical analysis of elections and polls. People who practice psephology are called psephologists.

SKILLS REQUIRED:
  • Writing and Research Skills
  • Public Speaking and Presentation Skills
  • Organisational skills
  • Time management skills
SUCCESS STORY

Sanjay Kumar

Sanjay Kumar, an Indian political analyst, and psephologist, was the director of the Centre for the Study of Developing Societies (CSDS) from 2014 to 2020. His focus areas include electoral politics, political mobilization, Indian youth, and democracy. With a background in Political Science, he conducted research on topics such as democracy in South Asia, Indian farmers, electoral violence, and Delhi's slums during his academic journey.

Entrepreneurship

How to be an entrepreneur in this field?

Provide strategic advice to political parties on campaign strategies, target demographics, and communication approaches.

SUCCESS STORY

Matt Singh

Matt Singh is an election and opinion polling analyst and was founder of the website and polling company Number Cruncher Politics, based in London. Singh rose to prominence in mid-2015 for his analysis which correctly predicted the opinion polling failure and Conservative victory at the 2015 UK general election.

For The Differently Abled

People with the following disabilities can work in this career
  • OL = one leg affected (R and/or L)
  • OA = one arm affected (R or L)
  • BL = both legs affected
  • HH = Hearing Handicapped (persons suffering from hearing impairment)
  • Low vision
  • Leprosy cured persons
